How I think

Curiosity, judgement and the confidence to make complexity useful.

I have always been interested in why things work, where they fail and how they could work better. Emergency Medicine made that instinct practical. It taught me to understand a complicated system quickly, separate signal from noise and make thoughtful decisions when certainty is impossible.

Medicine and strategy ask the same questions: what’s really happening, what matters most, and what should we do next? I use that approach beyond the clinic now. I challenge assumptions, find the real problem, and turn ideas into strategies and products that work in real life. AI helps me think faster and test ideas sooner. Judgement still leads.
