Privacy

How Betty Pro uses your information

Last updated: 12 September 2026.

When you request Betty Pro, this site collects your email address so the prompt and practical guide can be delivered to you. Your email address is also used to send occasional updates about improving how you work with AI. It is not collected for unrelated purposes.

What is stored

The subscriber record contains your normalised email address, subscription status, signup source, consent time, prompt version, delivery status and relevant record timestamps. Betty Pro does not ask for your name, occupation or the content of your AI conversations.

To understand whether the resource is useful, the site may count page visits, prompt views, copies and downloads. Any anonymous identifier used for this purpose lasts only for the current page view and is not stored in your browser. See “Website analytics” below for how Google Analytics 4 is used, with your consent, across the rest of the site.

The application does not store raw IP addresses or user-agent strings in its subscriber or analytics tables. It uses short-lived, hashed request fingerprints to enforce rate limits. Hosting, network and email providers may process standard request metadata under their own operational and security policies.

Website analytics

With your consent, this site uses Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to measure aggregate usage — pages viewed, how visitors move around the site, and anonymised heatmaps of where people scroll and click — so I can see what is useful and improve it. Both stay off until you actively choose “Allow analytics” in the consent banner shown on your first visit; declining, or taking no action, keeps them off. Your choice lasts 13 months, after which you are asked again, and you can change it at any time using the “Privacy choices” link next to the Privacy Policy link in the footer of every page.

Google Analytics never receives your name, email address, the content of the contact form, the Betty Pro signup, or anything else you type into a form on this site — only anonymised, aggregate usage data. Your IP address is anonymised before Google Analytics processes it. Advertising storage (ad_storage) is always denied: this is measurement only, never advertising, and analytics data is never used to build advertising profiles or shared for marketing purposes.

Microsoft Clarity records anonymised heatmaps and session replays — the shape of how visitors scroll, click and move through the pages — for the same purpose of understanding what is useful. It is subject to the same consent choice as Google Analytics: it stays off until you choose “Allow analytics”, and if you decline or later withdraw, it is turned off and its cookies cleared. Session replays are masked: every form and input on the site is tagged so Clarity records interaction shapes only, never the text you type — it never captures your name, email address, the contents of the contact form, the Betty Pro signup, or anything else you enter. Advertising is never involved.

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga) and Microsoft Clarity sets cookies (such as _clck and _clsk) to distinguish visits; declining, or later withdrawing consent, means no analytics cookies are set, and any already set are cleared immediately.

Who processes it

Email sign-ups are handled by Kit, which stores your email address and sends our emails.

Access and retention

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also email hello@drlizlondon.com to be removed.

Your choices

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity run only if you accept cookies.

You may request access to or deletion of your subscriber information by emailing hello@drlizlondon.com or using the contact form on the main site.

Legal review recommended: confirm the final retention schedule, postal contact details and data-controller information before expanding email marketing.
