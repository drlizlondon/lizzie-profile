Privacy

How Betty Pro uses your information

When you request Betty Pro, this site collects your email address so the prompt and practical guide can be delivered to you. Your email address is also used to send occasional updates about improving how you work with AI. It is not collected for unrelated purposes.

What is stored

The subscriber record contains your normalised email address, subscription status, signup source, consent time, prompt version, delivery status and relevant record timestamps. Betty Pro does not ask for your name, occupation or the content of your AI conversations.

To understand whether the resource is useful, the site also counts page visits, prompt views, copies and downloads. A random identifier is stored in your browser and hashed by the server before an analytics record is saved. These aggregate records contain an allowed event name, a broad page label, counts and timestamps. They are not linked to your email address and are not used to follow you across other sites.

The application does not store raw IP addresses or user-agent strings in its subscriber or analytics tables. It uses short-lived, hashed request fingerprints to enforce rate limits. Hosting, network and email providers may process standard request metadata under their own operational and security policies.

Who processes it

The Betty backend and owner dashboard run on OpenAI Sites. Subscriber, consent, delivery, unsubscribe, security and aggregate usage records are stored in the Sites project’s Cloudflare D1 database. Resend processes delivery of the prompt and guide by email. The public portfolio is hosted separately on Vercel. Each provider’s handling of data is governed by its own privacy terms and the agreements in place with the site operator.

Access and retention

Subscriber records and aggregate usage counts are available only through the restricted owner dashboard or the underlying service administration tools. Minimal consent and unsubscribe records are retained so that an unsubscribe request can continue to be honoured. Retention periods and the final deletion-request process must be confirmed as part of the legal setup noted below.

Your choices

Every Betty email includes a secure unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops future Betty updates while retaining a minimal record of the consent and unsubscribe request. If you request the prompt again after unsubscribing, the delivery email may be sent, but you will not be re-enrolled in future updates unless you give consent again through a future explicit process.

You may request access to or deletion of your subscriber information through the contact route on the main site once its verified contact address has been added.

Required legal completion: the main site still contains a placeholder contact address, and no verified privacy contact email, postal address, retention schedule or fuller data-controller details have been supplied. These details and the deletion-request process must be added and reviewed before this notice is treated as production-ready or Betty updates are marketed.
